`docs/`, which is ignored by Git. Keep generated research data and registries
under ignored `data/`. Future publication exports must use a separate explicitly
chosen folder. Do not commit generated notes, research data or credentials.

`docs/phase2/`. Preserve existing model artifacts and sealed forecasts.
